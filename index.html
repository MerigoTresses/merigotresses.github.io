A Trusted Braider

Welcome to Merigo Tresses — a warm, home-based African braiding studio in Fukutsu, Fukuoka, Japan. I'm Mercy, a passionate braider dedicated to creating neat, beautiful, and long-lasting protective styles for the African diaspora community here in Japan.

From classic box braids to elegant knotless styles, every braid is crafted with care, precision, and a gentle touch. Whether you're looking for an everyday protective style or something special for an occasion, I'd love to bring your hair vision to life.
