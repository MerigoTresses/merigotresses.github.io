✦ About Merigo Tresses

A Trusted Braider
in Fukuoka

Welcome to Merigo Tresses — a warm, home-based braiding studio nestled right here in Fukuoka, Japan. I'm Mercy, a passionate braider dedicated to creating neat, beautiful, and long-lasting protective styles tailored to your unique hair and vision.

From classic box braids to elegant knotless styles, every braid is crafted with care, precision, and a gentle touch. Whether you're looking for an everyday protective style or something special for an occasion, I'd love to bring your hair vision to life — right from the comfort of my home studio.
